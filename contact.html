Contact

Program questions and research inquiries travel different routes. This page explains which one to use and what to include. Academy contact details will be published here when they are confirmed.

Program questions

For prospective learners, families, teachers, and school staff asking about the K–12 STEM, College & Research, or Professional Learning path.

Helpful to include

  • The path you are asking about, for example “K–12 STEM”.
  • Your year level or role, in general terms.
  • Whether you want to be told when a program opens.

Contact details for program questions: to be published.

Research inquiries

For faculty, students, and engineers who want to discuss a proposed theme, propose a collaboration, or ask a question about a research direction.

Helpful to include

  • The theme you mean: resilient computing, space communications, or long-term digital preservation.
  • The question you would like to investigate, in one or two sentences.
  • Your institution or organisation, if relevant.

Contact details for research inquiries: to be published.

About this page

There is no contact form here. A form that cannot deliver a message is worse than no form at all, so the Academy will publish confirmed contact details on this page instead. Until then, nothing on this site can send a message to the Academy.

Please do not send student records, identification documents, or other personal data through any channel until a published Academy address explains how it is handled.
