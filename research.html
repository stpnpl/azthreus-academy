Research

Proposed research themes connected to the engineering interests of Azthreus Systems. Each theme is explained in plain language and paired with one research question. Nothing on this page is a published result.

Proposed, not published

The Academy has no published findings, funded studies, or completed experiments. Text marked Proposed describes work the Academy intends to pursue. If a theme produces results, the paper or report would be linked from this page and labelled as a publication.

01

Resilient computing

A computer running far from Earth is hit by radiation that can flip a single bit in memory. The program keeps running, but one value is now wrong. Resilience research asks how a system notices that kind of silent damage, corrects it, and shows that the correction worked — without a technician on site.

In plain terms: the interesting problem is not only surviving the fault, but being able to say afterwards what happened and why the state that remains is trustworthy.

Research question

How can a system prove that it recovered correctly from a fault it never detected when the fault occurred?

  • Fault tolerance
  • Redundancy
  • Verification
  • Proposed
02

Space communications

Across interplanetary distances a message can take minutes or hours to arrive, and the link can close before the reply. Store-and-forward networking handles this by letting each node hold data until the next contact window. The hard part is trust: the receiver gets a file in pieces, long after the sender has gone quiet, and has to decide whether the result is the file that was sent.

This theme is about the evidence a receiver can gather — digests, sequence records, custody reports — and what each piece of evidence actually rules out.

Research question

How can a receiver verify that a file is intact after a long interruption?

  • Delay-tolerant networking
  • Store-and-forward
  • Integrity verification
  • Proposed
03

Long-term digital preservation

A file that opens today may not open in a hundred years, not because the bits were lost but because the software that reads it no longer exists. Preservation research works on keeping records understandable across that gap: checking that stored data has not changed, migrating formats before they become unreadable, and documenting how a file was meant to be read.

The practical question is one of priorities. Every choice — keep the original, keep an accurate copy, keep the tools that interpret it — costs something and protects something different.

Research question

What minimal set of records lets someone a century from now confirm that an archived file is unchanged and interpret what it means?

  • Digital preservation
  • Fixity
  • Format migration
  • Proposed

Where this stands

How proposed work becomes published work

A theme on this page is a direction, not a result. Turning one into research means writing a question precise enough to be wrong, building something that can produce evidence, and reporting the outcome even when it is inconvenient.

The Academy would publish methods and results openly, including negative results, and would credit learners who contributed to a study. No timeline has been set, because no study has been approved or funded.

How themes connect to Azthreus Systems

The three themes follow the company's engineering interests: radiation-tolerant operating systems, delay-tolerant networking, and long-term archival. The Academy studies the questions behind that work at a pace and depth a product schedule cannot always allow.

What we will not claim

No partnerships, funding, publication venues, or supervisory relationships are asserted on this site. Each will be stated plainly here when it exists.
